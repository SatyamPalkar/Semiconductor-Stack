# Semiconductor value chain — initial research map

Central question: where is value created and captured, and how could that change?

The initial roster separates NVIDIA (design/computing platforms), TSMC (foundry), ASML (lithography equipment), Micron (memory), Texas Instruments (analog/embedded) and Amkor (assembly/packaging/test). These are scope categories, not an estimate of market share or a claim of exclusive supplier relationships. See company investor-relations links in the source catalog. Company financial coverage beyond NVIDIA is pending.

Investigate differences in demand cyclicality, asset intensity, technical bottlenecks, customer concentration and pricing power. The AI-computing case should trace evidence across design, manufacturing and packaging without adding supplier/customer revenues as market size. Geographic and regulatory claims need official sourced documents before becoming findings.

No industry valuation, ranking or investment recommendation is concluded from the one-company financial pilot.
