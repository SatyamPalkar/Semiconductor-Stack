# NVIDIA financial pilot

Source: NVIDIA's official fourth-quarter and FY2025 earnings release, stated publication date 26 February 2025. It presents unaudited US GAAP annual columns ending 26 January 2025 and 28 January 2024, in USD millions. The committed fixture retains only selected financial rows and headers; complete original bytes remain in ignored local raw storage.

FY2025 reported revenue is 130,497 and operating cash flow is 64,089 (USD millions). Release-defined FCF is calculated as 64,089 less 3,236 capital purchases and 129 related principal payments = 60,724. The metric API and snapshot expose the individual source inputs. No conclusions about durability, intrinsic value or prospective returns follow from those figures alone.
